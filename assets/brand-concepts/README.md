# Akra Dev icon concepts

Generated on 2026-08-18 with the built-in `image_gen` workflow.

Concept 01, Forward-Cut A, was selected for production. Its approved raster is retained here as the reference; the transparent runtime assets are generated from `scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs` into `public/brand/`.

## Brand identity used

Akra Dev is an own-product-first product team, not a generic development vendor. It plans, builds, launches, operates, and improves its own products, then selectively joins partnerships where its technology is central to the business. The shared promise across WAXBALL, Akra Quick Translate, and WhyShutdown is turning a concrete problem into a working, operable product and proving the result with real evidence.

The concepts use the Akra design system's Midnight Product Gallery palette:

- Midnight canvas: `#09090B`
- Product blue: `#3B82F6`
- Restrained light accent: `#75D9FF` or white
- Strong silhouette and generous optical margin for 16–32 px use

Avoided motifs: code brackets, gears, wrenches, rockets, AI sparkles, circuit traces, laptops, and WAXBALL-specific imagery.

## Concepts and prompt set

### 01 — Forward-Cut A

Evolve the existing geometric A into a bolder two-plane symbol. A single negative-space path moves from the lower-left through the center and exits forward, representing a problem becoming a working product and being shipped. Keep it centered, crisp, minimal, and readable at small sizes.

File: `akra-dev-icon-01-forward-cut-a.png`

### 02 — Built Core

Three thick front-facing structural plates interlock around one white square core. The structure is stable and operational, with one deliberate opening suggesting release. Avoid an isometric cube or blockchain-like construction.

File: `akra-dev-icon-02-built-core.png`

### 03 — 80/20 Orbit

A dominant four-fifths open ring represents Akra's own-product work, while a separate one-fifth rectangular facet represents selective technical partnerships. The empty center is a product stage. Avoid loading, refresh, pie-chart, clock, or planet readings.

File: `akra-dev-icon-03-80-20-orbit.png`

### 04 — Proof Beam

A hard-edged white beam reveals one compact blue product block on the midnight stage. The negative space may suggest an A, but the product block remains the proof and focal point. Avoid literal spotlight fixtures, stars, sparkles, eyes, and apertures.

File: `akra-dev-icon-04-proof-beam.png`

### 05 — Ship Loop

Four thick modules form one continuous square spiral for problem definition, design, build, and operation. The final upper-right module opens outward at 45 degrees to suggest release and continued improvement; the center remains a small diamond-shaped negative space.

File: `akra-dev-icon-05-ship-loop.png`

## Shared generation constraints

Square 1:1 brand/app icon; one centered original mark; solid midnight backdrop; flat vector-like geometry; no text or wordmark; no mockup, border, watermark, decorative texture, tiny detail, or product-specific motif.

These PNGs are concept-selection assets, not final vector masters. After choosing a direction, redraw it as deterministic SVG, create light/dark and monochrome variants, and test at 16, 24, 32, 64, and 512 px.
