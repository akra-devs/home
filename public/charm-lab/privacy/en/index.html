Charm Lab Privacy Policy

Version: 1.1.0 · Effective date: 2026-09-22

About Charm Lab

AKRA provides Charm Lab, a digital charm decorating and matching game for a general audience aged 13 and older. Contact: help@akra.kr. This policy covers the Android and iOS app; platform availability and advertising approval are separate from this notice.

Information kept on your device

Your designs, charm ownership, bead balance, matching-game progress, reward records, and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not require an account or upload this information to an AKRA server. There is no cloud synchronization. For advertising settings, the app saves only a selected age band (under 13, 13–17, or 18 and older), not your birth date.

Optional advertising

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). After an age band is selected, UMP checks the privacy choices required in your region. Ads are requested only when UMP permits them and you choose the optional bonus after a rewarded game round. Declining, closing, or being unable to load an ad does not remove your base reward or block play.

Google may process IP-based approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. These data are sent to Google, not stored in an AKRA player account. Google's privacy and retention practices apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. No separate Analytics or Crashlytics service is enabled by Charm Lab.

Age and privacy choices

Under-13 selections receive no ads. Ages 13–17 receive Google's teen treatment with a PG maximum ad content rating; adults also receive a PG maximum. This content setting does not replace regional consent requirements. You can change the age band in Settings. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings also provides Advertising privacy choices. Device advertising-ID controls are available in operating-system settings.

Motion and sharing

Accelerometer and gyroscope readings make charms sway while the studio is visible. They are processed transiently on the device and are not saved or transmitted by Charm Lab. Tilt response can be disabled. On iOS, motion access is explained by the motion permission notice. The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

When you choose Share, the exported PNG is passed to the operating-system share sheet. The service you select handles it under its own policy.

Retention and deletion

Local designs and progression remain until you reset app data, clear system app storage, or uninstall; recovery is not guaranteed. The in-app reset removes designs and progression but retains preferences and privacy choices. A local backup of the previous wallet format can be retained for migration recovery. Clearing system app storage or uninstalling removes local app storage subject to operating-system backup behavior. Files shared outside the app must be deleted separately.

Google controls retention of advertising data under its own policy. Contact help@akra.kr for privacy questions or requests; we cannot remotely retrieve or restore a device-only wallet. If you contact us by email, your address and message are used to respond to your request.

Updates

We update this notice when app behavior changes. The current notice is available offline in Settings and at the linked public policy page. Earlier policy versions remain in the project's policy archive.
