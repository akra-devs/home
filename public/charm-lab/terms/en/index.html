Charm Lab Usage Terms

Version: 1.1.0 · Effective date: 2026-09-22

Charm Lab is a creative decorating and matching game for a general audience aged 13 and older. AKRA support: help@akra.kr.

Beads are free device-local progression currency with no cash value. They cannot be bought, transferred, traded, or redeemed. There are no paid products or in-app purchases in this version.

Completing each of the first three rewarded matching rounds in a local day grants 40 beads. The optional rewarded ad for each such round can grant 40 additional beads only after the SDK reports completion. Practice rounds remain available afterward. Ad availability is not guaranteed. Closing or failing to load an ad preserves the base reward. Rewards and unlocks are saved before the interface reports success; if saving fails, retry the save as directed. An earned callback not yet written to device storage can be lost if the process terminates.

Charms display a fixed bead price and require an explicit unlock action. Unlocking a charm keeps it available for future designs. Built-in art may be used in personal images exported from the app; this does not grant permission to redistribute the app source or original asset collection separately.

Designs and progress are stored on the device without cloud recovery and can be lost following data deletion, uninstall, or storage failure. Keep important designs as exported PNG files. Operating-system backup behavior may affect restored local data.
